Personal Statement

Art is Reinterpretation.

Computer Science student during class hours, visual artist and video editor in the studio.

I believe art is not restricted to traditional mediums. Whether it is a brand mark for a restaurant, a poster for Tamil cinema, a short video edit of a motor racing lap, or a stage event for college drama, I approach every project by absorbing the existing narrative and executing it with my own visual language.

During my time at Chennai Institute of Technology, I co-founded the official college drama club IMMERSE, designed its visual identity and mask logomark, and helped build its presence from scratch. I also joined the media team for our annual cultural fest Takshashila in my first year and went on to lead the entire festival media team in subsequent years, directing live footage capture, promotional teaser edits, and visual graphics.

When I take on freelance clients, my objective is simple: deliver sharp, non-generic work tailored precisely to the vision without filler or artificial fluff.

YEAR 1 : MEDIA TEAM MEMBER

Operated camera gear on-ground, logged raw footage clips, and edited rapid daily social media recap reels during fest hours.

YEAR 2 & 3 : MEDIA TEAM HEAD

Directed a multi-member camera crew, assigned coverage schedules across stage events, established video color profiles, and managed post-production turnaround for mainstage teasers.
